== Confirmed bugs in the generated PDFs (disposition `bug`)

* E.118 A, C, R: the contact labels Tel/Fax/E-mail and the country name are printed in English; the
  official PDFs translate them (the dataset holds English only for these).
* E.118-E, row `89 1 432`: the company cell drops "WORTHINGTON, OH 43085" at a page break.
* E.164B-S, E.164D E/F/S/A/R: the alphabetical lists sort accented names (Cote d'Ivoire, Belarus, Benin,
  Brunei, Canada, Virgenes ...) after the unaccented ones; the official lists collate by base letter.
  (The generated list puts "Reserved" entries at R; the official puts them last: layout.)

== Data findings (disposition `data`: the generated PDF faithfully prints the dataset, the official differs)

E.118 F/S/E: the rows sort by the English country name where the official PDF sorts by the language's own
name. E.164C: Malaisie/Malawi names swapped (265/60), prefixes 11/1002/1008 vs official 011/001,002/001,008,
utc "0" vs "UTC". E.164D F/S: the Antigua row carries the Bahamas names. E.164B F/S: duplicate Canada row
instead of Etats-Unis, Bonaire twice instead of Curacao, Kazakhstan twice.

== Prose

One rule only, checked on the samples of E.118-S, E.164B-S, E.180-F, E.180-S: when the prose deltas of a row,
taken together as a word bag, differ only in page numbers (up to two digits), the Spanish "N.º" and the
"ITU-T" hyphenation, they are `layout` (footers and line breaks). Rows with any other word difference keep
all prose deltas unreviewed: title pages, "Note from TSB" blocks (present only in the official PDFs of
E.164D) and "(position on ...)" lines are real content differences, counted open, not classified.

== E.180 (time-boxed, not settled)

Only the layout rule (same words in both rows) is applied. The data/bug rules were tried and withdrawn:
they read footnote letters ("a)") as another language's text and the rows are multi-line tone cells, so a
bug/data claim was not supported by the samples. Remaining: wrapped tone cells, stranded country headings
(Cuba, Japan), dropped footnote markers. Counted open.
