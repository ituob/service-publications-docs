= F.32 completeness: printed-source search (G11)

== Result

No consolidated printed F.32 list was found in the searched `itu-ob-data`
checkout. The references identify the _List of Telegram Destination Indicators_,
in accordance with ITU-T Recommendation F.32 (10/1995), published with OB 980,
position on 15 May 2011. They do not contain its country and office rows.

The recovered 1,214-row list described in the task has therefore not been
independently checked against that printed edition. Matching, missing and extra
row counts are *undetermined*, not zero. This search does not establish that the
recovered list is complete.

== Remaining evidence needed

15 May 2011, is needed before country-by-country and row-by-row counts can be
reported. The recovered seed and replay output cannot provide that independent
evidence. The negative result is limited to the three locations and revision
above; it does not assert that the printed edition is unavailable elsewhere.

Following the task's no-source stopping condition, no comparison script or
two-country diff spec was created. No seed, corpus snapshot or baseline was
changed.
