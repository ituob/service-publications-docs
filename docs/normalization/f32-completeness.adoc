= F.32 printed-source seed completeness

== Source and result

The OB 980 F.32/TDI annex is available as an independently published ITU PDF:
`https://www.itu.int/dms_pub/itu-t/opb/sp/T-SP-F.32-2011-PDF-E.pdf`. The checked-in
fixture is `ituob/spec/fixtures/f32/official-2011.pdf` (SHA-256
`7ef59fbbfcddd5690dd4f5e2de87a618e31898c8943ca1335c6c4e2a4556c3d9`) and its
extracted source is `ituob/spec/fixtures/f32/official-2011.xml`. The publication
date is 2011-05-15; the PDF has 47 pages.

`datasets/980-F.32/publication.yaml` is regenerated by
`scripts/build_f32_seed_publication.rb`. It records 2,321 source rows, 1,092 unique
four-letter office allocations, and 1,140 separately classified context rows.
Those contexts include network and country headings, offices with network-relative
suffixes, shared indicators, aliases, telex references, reservations, continuations,
and printed rows without an assigned indicator. Ambiguous suffixes are not expanded
into invented codes. An unclassified source row makes the builder fail; a successful
build marks the publication source complete and preserves the earlier seed rows and
their annotations as provenance.

Printed offices whose suffix is typed at the end of the name cell ("Baguio --BG") are
parsed as their own rows. The artifact is mandatory: a missing or unreadable
`publication.yaml` fails validation and never falls back to the unverified legacy
`data.yaml`. OB 988 repeats an indicator across several printed offices; the first
printed row is the register entry (as in the seed) and each further printed row is
kept as a shared-indicator context, so all 473 printed rows are retained. That REP
replaces the whole Egypt list, so it carries `replaces_network: true` in its change
data. The change source withdraws every office the register holds on that network at
that issue (seed and earlier amendments) that the REP does not print. The printed
description does not decide this; a flagged file with no entries or no network fails
validation naming the file.

The corrected seed removes the previous false missing-seed interpretation for
Malaysia at OB 1013 and Ukraine at OB 1148.
The Malaysia and Ukraine withdrawals apply without replay errors. The separate
context-only action remains recorded with its source context; it is not counted as
an allocation.

== Reproduction and limits

Run `BUNDLE_GEMFILE=ituob/Gemfile bundle exec ruby scripts/build_f32_seed_publication.rb`
from the service repository root to regenerate the typed LML publication. The
focused parser, source-adapter, provider, and replay checks are under
`ituob/spec/ituob/models/f32_seed_builder_spec.rb`,
`ituob/spec/ituob/registers/f32_directory_change_source_spec.rb`, and
`ituob/spec/ituob/registers/source_gap_replay_spec.rb`.

The source PDF establishes the printed OB 980 edition. It does not claim that later
replay snapshots are independent evidence of the seed, and it does not resolve the
separate X.121 206 1, Q.708 misfiled-event, NNP, or List VIII source gaps.

== Comparison with the recovered 1,214-row list

The generated comparison below uses the independently parsed PDF/XML as its
reference, not the replay or the generated `publication.yaml`. Its candidate is
`ituob/spec/fixtures/f32/recovered-1214.json`, reproduced from recovery commit
`d75860e4e5f8b5e3e70275ac64ff814f083ef54e` using that revision's composite
`country_or_area`, `network_code`, `office_code` keys. The JSON SHA-256 is
`2afc40eb22639405f917a10e36118e95d263c81cd0d125d7c8f8b907d2154f22`.
The historical replay reports three skipped amendments (OB 1004, 1013 and 1148);
those defects are retained in this candidate rather than silently repaired.

This candidate is the recovered *current* state through OB 1148, not a 2011
seed edition. Its differences from the printed OB 980 edition can include later
amendments and extraction defects. The 816-row `datasets/980-F.32/data.yaml`
is the original partial seed, not the recovered 1,214-row list. Context rows
without office codes are counted separately; coded rows are compared without
deduplicating the recovered data. These counts therefore do not certify the
recovered state as a complete or correct 2011 seed.

Regenerate the comparison (including every missing, extra and different row):

[source,sh]
----

----

the service repository root (the historical revision must be available locally):
